About KAAPA & Important Disclaimer

KAAPA (Your Loyal APK Guardian) is a lightweight, privacy-first safety watchdog built to translate complex Android app internals into simple, everyday English before you tap "Install".

Important Safety Notice KAAPA is an educational and static permission/tracker inspector — not a replacement for Google Play Protect or full antivirus software. Always download apps from trusted stores and keep Google Play Protect enabled on your phone.

Why We Built KAAPA

  • Zero Technical Jargon: Instead of cryptic code names, KAAPA barks clearly if an app can read your bank OTPs, watch your screen, or track your GPS location.
  • 100% On-Device Privacy: Your APK files are unpacked and inspected strictly inside your own browser—nothing is ever uploaded to any cloud server.
  • Instant Trust Signals: Generates a SHA-256 digital fingerprint, detects 18+ hidden ad/analytics trackers, and lets you export a clean PDF safety report.

How KAAPA Sniffs Your APK

Every time you drop an .apk (or split .xapk / .apkm) file into KAAPA, our watchdog runs 10 static safety checks in seconds:

  • Permission Audit: Checks 18 sensitive permissions including SMS reading, Accessibility Service screen control, overlay popups, microphone, camera, and background GPS.
  • Hidden Ad & Analytics Trackers: Scans the app's compiled code for 18 third-party advertising and tracking SDKs (Meta/Facebook, Google AdMob, Firebase, Unity Ads, AppsFlyer, Adjust, and more).
  • Signature & Build Hygiene: Verifies whether a classic signing stamp is present and flags unfinished debuggable test builds or unencrypted http:// web links.
  • SHA-256 Fingerprint: Computes the exact cryptographic hash so you can cross-check the file against 70+ antivirus engines on VirusTotal with one click.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is built into KAAPA's architecture by design:

  • No File Uploads: All APK extraction, manifest parsing, bytecode scanning, and SHA-256 hashing happen locally in your browser's memory. Your APK files never leave your device.
  • No Tracking Cookies: KAAPA only uses your browser's localStorage to remember your chosen background coat theme.
  • Support & Complaint Messages: If you choose to send a message via Bone Menu → Bark at Host / Report Issue, only the email address, message, and optional scan summary you submit are transmitted to the host so we can respond to your feedback.
